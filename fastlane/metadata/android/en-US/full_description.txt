Sancta is a companion for your prayer life and for preparing for confession. It is free, with no ads, no account and no tracking: everything you record stays on your phone.

<b>Garden of the Soul</b>
Every day of prayer makes your garden bloom: one patch of lawn per day, greener and fuller with perseverance. The Rosary, Mass, confession and novenas each have their own flowers.

<b>Daily prayer plan</b>
Plan your prayers for morning, noon and night, tick off what you prayed and set reminders at the times you prefer.

<b>Guided Holy Rosary</b>
Pray bead by bead with the mysteries of the day, a vibration for each Hail Mary, a long vibration at the end of each mystery and a celebration when you finish.

<b>Prayers and novenas</b>
Traditional prayers with search by name (some also in Latin) and novenas with a daily reminder.

<b>Examination of conscience</b>
Daily examination, confession journey and confession guide, protected by a password. Your data stays on the device.

<b>Guides</b>
Guide to Mass (ordinary and traditional form), a Eucharistic Adoration outline and your prayer intentions.

<b>App guide</b>
A step-by-step tour shows where everything is.

<b>Free, made with love</b>
The app was made by a couple preparing for marriage, to help with wedding expenses and so that Catholics can pray well and live their faith every day. If you'd like to help, you will find PIX, card (Ko-fi) and crypto in "About the Project" in the menu. A prayer for us is already a great gift.
